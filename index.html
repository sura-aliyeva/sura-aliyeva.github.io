FEATURED · ENVIRONMENTAL ANALYTICS

Methane Intelligence — Global & Azerbaijan Emissions Monitoring

Which methane detections can we trace to a source, and which sources warrant a closer look? I used satellite observations from UNEP’s International Methane Emissions Observatory (IMEO) to study global coverage, then examined source identification, emission rates and repeat detections in Azerbaijan’s oil and gas sector.

Data source · Methane Alert and Response System (MARS)

An independent environmental analytics project supporting measurement, reporting and verification (MRV) decisions. It is separate from official OGMP 2.0 reporting.

Why the Power BI count fell short

In Power BI, the source filter context excluded 10 unassigned observations, leaving 145 visible. I counted all Azerbaijan oil and gas observations in SQL, then grouped them by source assignment. The two groups reconcile to 155.

93.55% of observations had an identified source (145 of 155).

Technical architecture, methodology & report pages

From observation to report

  1. Prepare and explore. Python, Pandas and NumPy support ETL and exploratory data analysis of MARS observations, source assignments and measured flux.
  2. Model and validate. The project uses 11 PostgreSQL tables. SQL checks distinguish observation counts from distinct sources and keep unassigned detections in the denominator.
  3. Analyze and report. Statistical analysis summarizes emission rates and source persistence. Power BI and DAX present the findings across four report pages.
